\#Process

1\. Cleaned and prepared the data by standardizing neighborhood names (removing inconsistent capitalization and extra spaces) and replacing missing bedroom values with to represent studio apartments.



2\. Analyzed neighborhood popularity by creating pivot tables using the number of reviews in the last 12 months to identify the top 10 most attractive neighborhoods for vacation rentals.



3\. Determined the most popular property sizes by creating additional pivot tables to identify the preferred number of bedrooms overall and within each of the top 10 neighborhoods.



4\. Created visualizations to communicate findings, including a bar chart displaying the most attractive neighborhoods based on review activity.



5\. Identified top-performing listings by filtering properties that matched the recommended neighborhood and bedroom-size combinations and creating a top\_listing indicator.



6\. Calculated rental revenue by using the calendar dataset to determine nightly revenue for booked dates and summing 30-day earnings for each listing with the SUMIF() function.



7\. Estimated annual revenue by multiplying each listing's 30-day revenue by 12 and ranking the recommended listings based on projected earnings.



8\. Finalized the project by documenting data cleaning steps, assumptions, and key insights, organizing the workbook with an executive summary and table of contents, and applying consistent formatting throughout the analysis.

\#Assumptions

* The top three most attractive neighborhoods are Lower East Side, Hell's Kitchen, and Harlem.
* Studios, 1-bedrooms, and 2-bedrooms are the most popular property sizes.
* Out of the top 10 most attractive neighborhoods Midtown is the only one whose most popular property size is a 1-bedroom.
* listing 49946551 had the highest revenue per listing for the 30 day period.



\#Findings

Based on the guess reviews from the past 12 months, ten neighborhoods were noted as the most attractive for vacations rentals: Lower East Side, Hell's Kitchen, Harlem, Midtown, Upper West Side, Chelsea, East Village, East Harlem, West Village, and Upper East Side.



The most popular property size among the top ten neighborhoods was a 1-bedroom, with Midtown being an exception where studios were the most common. Overall the most popular property sizes were studios, 1-bedroom, and 2-bedrooms.



The top listings generated $5,474,281.00 collectively, over a 30 day period. The listing with the highest revenue in the 30 day period from September 7th, 2022 to October 6th, 2022 was 49946551, which generated $29,940.
